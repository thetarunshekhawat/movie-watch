Before you start — read this
  • Wear headphones. Otherwise the movie's audio goes into your mic and echoes back to the other person. This can't be fixed in software.
  • You both need the same file. Ideally the exact same download — the app warns you if they differ.
  • If the movie won't load, it's a codec problem. The error message will give you the exact ffmpeg command to fix it.
  • Use Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari support fewer video formats.
